Privacy Policy

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used by its owner to upload YouTube Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public. Last updated: November 23, 2026.

What the app accesses

The app requests only the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload). It uses this permission solely to upload videos that the owner chooses, with the titles and descriptions the owner enters, to the owner's own channels.

What data is stored

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal data. Authorization tokens are stored only in the owner's private GitHub repository secrets and are used only to perform the uploads described above. Videos are sent to the owner's private repository temporarily and are deleted automatically after upload.

Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is not transferred to third parties and is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
